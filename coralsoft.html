Our Story

Made in Zimbabwe.
Soft for the world.

CoralSoft is made by Supreme Brands (Pvt) Ltd, a tissue manufacturer with operations in Harare, Bulawayo, Masvingo, and Gweru. In Harare, we also operate from two Willowvale offices: Gleneagles Industrial Center Bay 1, 119 Dagenham Road, and 514 Reform Way near Mashwede Village.

Our range includes Sapphire, Classic, Aura, Elegance, Premium, Blue Blossom, Pink Blossom, Coral Soft Touch, pocket tissues, kitchen towels, and serviettes — all crafted for local trust, quality, and comfort.
